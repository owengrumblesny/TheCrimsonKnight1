# Earth-RED visual bible

## Premise
Owen Grumbles is the Crimson Knight, a fictional armored vigilante. Owen Enterprises builds the CR-X9 war suit, CR-X1 Interceptor, CR-B1 Batcycle and CR-A1 Specter. Red Citadel is the headquarters under New Gotham.

## Art direction
Crimson 0x990000, oxblood 0x660000, gunmetal 0x303030, black 0x111111, emissive red 0xff2020. Original angular industrial armor; long ear-like helmet fins, full faceplate, no exposed mouth. Distinctive OE monogram. Nighttime wet streets, gothic towers, industrial docks, dark caverns.

## Gameplay pillars
Free roam first, no required story; seamless access to vehicles and headquarters; tactile animation; expressive traversal; optional encounters and self-directed sandbox tools.

## Districts
Old Gotham, Downtown, Industrial, Narrows, Blackwater Island.

## Art pipeline
Blockout -> original concept -> high-poly -> low-poly -> UV -> PBR -> skeleton/skin -> animation clips -> collider/LOD -> glTF export -> runtime validation.
